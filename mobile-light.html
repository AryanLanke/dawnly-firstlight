Made for people who wake up before their alarm

You set an alarm for 5:30 AM, or midday, or before a night shift. You wake up early on your own, grab your phone, and start moving. Then at the scheduled time, the alarm blares anyway—waking the whole room or breaking your flow.

Dawnly fixes that. It works quietly with the alarms already in your Clock app at any hour, checking if you're active only in the window right before your alarm rings. When it sees you're up, it offers to dismiss it with a single tap. Plus, gentle micro-reminders help you build recurring waking habits throughout the day without disruptive noises.

How it works

Three steps, all on your phone. Nothing leaves it.

Reads your next alarm

Works with the Clock app you already use. Day or night, Dawnly never sets alarms of its own.

Watches only before it

Only in a custom window you pick, like 30 minutes before. Nothing runs in the background all night.

Asks before it acts

Screen use, steps, or movement show you're up. Dawnly asks, you tap, and the alarm is silenced peacefully.
